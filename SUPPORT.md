# Support

RoadLimit UAE is a community research beta, not a navigation authority, emergency service, legal service, or official Dubai/UAE application.

## Before requesting help

1. Read the [README](README.md), especially the platform limitations and store-release gates.
2. Use a development or release build; Expo Go cannot provide reliable background location.
3. Confirm that Precise Location and local notifications are enabled.
4. Reproduce only while parked, as a passenger, or in a controlled simulation.
5. Remove coordinates, home addresses, vehicle plates, faces, tokens, and diagnostic secrets.

## Where to ask

- App defect: use the GitHub **Bug report** issue form.
- Sourced road correction: use the **Road-limit correction** form.
- Feature proposal: use the **Feature request** form.
- General setup or usage question: use the **Usage question** form.
- Security or location-data issue: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately.

Please include the app version, device model, OS version, permissions state, foreground/background state, expected result, and redacted reproduction steps.

## Out of scope

The project cannot adjudicate fines, guarantee camera thresholds, replace road signs, provide emergency assistance, or advise anyone to exceed a posted limit. For an immediate road emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency authority.
