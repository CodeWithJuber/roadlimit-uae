# Code of Conduct

RoadLimit UAE contributors must help maintain a respectful, technically rigorous, and safety-conscious community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful across differences in background, experience, identity, and viewpoint.
- Critique evidence, code, and assumptions—not people.
- State uncertainty, disclose conflicts of interest, and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect location, identity, vehicle, and medical information shared accidentally.
- Collect road evidence only while safely parked or as a passenger.
- Keep safety-sensitive claims reproducible and source-backed.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate misinformation, unsafe driving instructions, publishing private trip data, and attempts to turn this project into a radar-evasion tool are not accepted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Submit a confidential report through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/CodeWithJuber/roadlimit-uae/security/advisories/new) and begin the title with **Conduct report**. If that form is unavailable, open a sanitized public issue requesting private contact; do not include names, screenshots, location data, or incident details.

The project aims to acknowledge reports within seven days. Maintainers should explain enforcement decisions privately where practical and avoid exposing reporters or affected people. Good-faith safety reports will not be penalized.
